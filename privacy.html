Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how MDO3D ("we", "us", "our") handles information across mdo3d.com and the family of platforms we operate (the "Services"). We aim to collect as little personal information as possible and to be clear about what we do collect.

Information we process

  • Information you provide. When you use a platform that requires input (for example, birth details for a chart or a question for a reading), we process that input to generate your result. For paid readings and tool results, that input is sent to Anthropic (see AI processing).
  • Account and billing data. If you create an account or purchase a paid feature, we process your email and authentication identifiers, and payment is handled by our processor, Stripe. We do not store full card details.
  • Usage data. Where you consent, we may use Google Analytics to understand how our Services are used and to improve them (see Cookies and analytics).

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve the Services, deliver results you request, process payments, respond to support requests, and keep the Services secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party processors

We rely on providers such as Firebase (authentication and database), Stripe (payments), Vercel (hosting), Anthropic, PBC (the Claude AI model that writes paid Guidance readings and the Rigor career tool results; see AI processing) and, if you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics to run the Services. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

AI processing

Paid readings and tool results are written by an AI model. When you request one:

  1. your input is sent over HTTPS to our API, hosted on Vercel;
  2. our API sends it to Anthropic, PBC, which runs the Claude model that writes the result;
  3. the result comes back to your browser.

Our API does not write these inputs to a database. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived request and error logs, which can include your checkout email when a payment completes. Anthropic processes the input to produce the result and keeps it only for the limited period its commercial terms allow; under those terms it does not use API inputs to train its models. Previews calculated in your browser (for example a basic chart, your core numbers, card meanings or symbol lookups) are not sent to Anthropic.

Guidance readings

This covers oracle, tarot, runes, I Ching, dreams, astrology, numerology, feng shui and past life on mdo3d.com subdomains.

  • What is sent to Anthropic. Only when you request the paid reading: your question and the cards, runes or hexagram drawn; for astrology, your birth date and, if you give them, birth time and place; for numerology, your full name and birth date; for dreams, your dream description; for feng shui, the details about your space you enter; for past life, your birth details and the clues you enter.
  • Checkout email. Before checkout we ask for your email. It goes to Stripe as your checkout email so Stripe can send your receipt. We do not email the reading itself; it is shown on the site after payment.
  • Unlocking. After payment, a one-time credit is saved in your browser's local storage on that site. It does not follow you to another browser or device. If you lose it, email hello@mdo3d.com with your checkout email and we will help.
  • Dream journal. Saved only in your browser's local storage. We never receive it.

Resume and career tools (Rigor)

The Rigor career tools at rigor.design (resume analyzer, cover letter, interview practice, LinkedIn profile, networking email, portfolio review and salary negotiation) work as follows:

  • Sign-in and plan. The tools require an account (email and password through Firebase Authentication) and an active Rigor Pro subscription. We keep a subscription record for your account (plan, status, renewal date and your Stripe customer and subscription IDs) and a monthly usage counter (the number of AI generations per tool). The counter holds counts only, not the text you entered.
  • What is sent. When you run a tool, the text you typed or pasted (for example your resume, a job description, a LinkedIn section, interview answers, or salary details) is sent over HTTPS to our API at api.rigor.design. The API trims it to a set length and forwards it to Anthropic, which generates the result. A resume file you choose to upload must be plain text (.txt); it is read in your browser and placed in the text box, and the file itself is never uploaded.
  • What we store. Our API does not write your resume or other tool input to our database or to our logs; it returns the result to your browser. Nothing is saved to your account unless you press Save. Saving stores a history item in our Firebase database, linked to your account and shown back to you in the tool: for the resume analyzer, the score plus the first 100 characters of your resume and of the job description; for the other tools, the generated result (for example the cover letter, email, or review) and the role or recipient details shown with it.
  • How long. Saved history items and usage counters are kept while your account exists. The subscription record is kept for as long as needed for billing and legal obligations. There is no in-app delete button yet: email hello@mdo3d.com from the address on your account and we will delete your saved history, or your whole account and its data, and confirm by email.
  • No sale, no profiling. We do not sell resume content, share it with employers or recruiters, or use it to build advertising profiles.

Cookies and analytics

Our sites can use Google Analytics 4, a Google service that counts visits and shows which pages are used. Where it is turned on, a banner asks first. Google Analytics loads only after you click Accept; it then sets its cookies (_ga and _ga_*, kept for up to two years) and sends usage data such as the pages you view, your device and browser type and your approximate location to Google. If you click Reject or don't answer, it does not load and no analytics cookie is set. We remember your choice in our own cookie (mdo_consent, 180 days); change it at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page. We don't use advertising cookies. A site where analytics is not turned on shows no banner and loads none of this.

Data retention

We keep account and billing records for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal obligations. Input used only to generate a one-time result is not retained beyond that purpose where feasible.

Your choices

Some of our tools work without an account. If you hold an account, you may request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us. See also our Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy and Acceptable Use Policy.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
