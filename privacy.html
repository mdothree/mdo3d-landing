Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how MDO3D ("we", "us", "our") handles information across mdo3d.com and the family of platforms we operate (the "Services"). We aim to collect as little personal information as possible and to be clear about what we do collect.

Information we process

  • Information you provide. When you use a platform that requires input (for example, birth details for a chart or a question for a reading), we process that input to generate your result.
  • Account and billing data. If you create an account or purchase a paid feature, we process your email and authentication identifiers, and payment is handled by our processor, Stripe. We do not store full card details.
  • Usage data. We may collect aggregate, non-identifying analytics to understand how our Services are used and to improve them.

How we use information

We use information to provide and improve the Services, deliver results you request, process payments, respond to support requests, and keep the Services secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party processors

We rely on providers such as Firebase (authentication), Stripe (payments), and Vercel (hosting) to run the Services. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms.

Data retention

We keep account and billing records for as long as your account is active and as needed to meet legal obligations. Input used only to generate a one-time result is not retained beyond that purpose where feasible.

Your choices

You can use many of our tools without an account. If you hold an account, you may request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@mdo3d.com.
